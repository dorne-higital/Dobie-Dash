# Notes for App Review (App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes)

No sign-in is needed. Open the app and tap the paw button to play. The first run is a short
tutorial.

ADS
Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always the player's choice (keep running after a crash,
double the biscuits from a run, open the free chest early). From the 3rd session there is
an interstitial at most every 3rd finished run; the "Remove Ads" purchase turns those off.
The App Tracking Transparency prompt appears just before the first ad, never at launch.

IN-APP PURCHASES
All are in Wardrobe → Shop. Remove Ads, Starter Pack, biscuit and Golden Bone packs
(consumable), Gold Collar, Pup Pass Season 1, Party Pack (three outfits), Every Breed and
single breeds (each breed can also be unlocked with 80 Golden Bones earned in play).
Restore Purchases is at the top of the Shop.

LEADERBOARDS
Leagues, the Daily Dash and Weekend Cups (League tab) run on our own server. Players join
automatically with an anonymous ID; there is no login. "Keep your place" offers Sign in
with Apple, which is optional and only keeps the same board name on a new phone. Each score
is checked by replaying the run on the server before it counts.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT
The only thing other players see is your dog's name and look. Names go through a word filter
on the server, and any name on a board can be reported (tap ••• next to it → Report name).
Reported names are hidden until reviewed.

GAME CENTER
Used only for the 30 achievements (bronze, silver and gold medals for 10 trophies).

To see a boss fight quickly: reach 1,000 points in Bark Park (about a minute).
