**Last updated: 21 November 2026**

Dobie Dash is a game made by Dan Horne ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the game
collects, why, and what you can do about it. I've kept it short and plain.

## The short version

- You don't need an account to play, and I never ask for your name.
- Your progress is saved on your phone and in your own iCloud.
- The leaderboards know your dog's name, how it looks and your scores, under an anonymous ID.
- Ads come from Google AdMob. They're only personalised if you say yes when iPhone asks
  about tracking.
- I use Google Firebase to count how the game is played and to find crashes.
- I never sell your data.

## What's collected, and why

**Your game save.** Biscuits, items, missions and settings are stored on your phone and in
your iCloud account (through Apple), so they move with you to a new phone. I can't see them.

**Leaderboards.** When you play online the game creates an anonymous player ID on my server
(Supabase, hosted in the EU). It stores your dog's name and look, your scores and the taps of
each scored run (so the server can replay the run and check it's real), your league, friends
you add by code, and any names you report. Other players see your dog's name, look and scores.

**Keep your place (optional).** If you choose to Sign in with Apple, Apple tells my server a
user ID and an email address (which can be Apple's private relay address). It's used only to
give you the same place on the boards on another phone. I don't email you.

**Purchases.** Apple handles all payments. RevenueCat (my purchase server) receives an
anonymous ID and what you bought, so purchases can be restored. I never see your card or
Apple ID.

**Ads.** Google AdMob shows ads. It may collect your device's advertising ID (only if you
allow tracking), approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads,
to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Analytics and crashes.** Google Firebase Analytics records game events such as a run
finishing or a world reached, with an app-instance ID, and Firebase Crashlytics sends crash
reports. These help me balance the game and fix bugs. They don't include your name.

**Game Center.** Achievements go through Apple's Game Center under your Game Center account.

**Feedback.** If you use Send feedback, you email me directly and I'll only use it to reply.

## Tracking

The first time an ad is about to show, iPhone asks whether Dobie Dash may track you. If you
say no, ads still show but aren't personalised, and no advertising ID is shared. You can
change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

## Children

Dobie Dash is rated 4+ but isn't made for children under 13 in particular. It doesn't ask
anyone for personal details. If you think a child has given me personal information, email
me and I'll delete it.

## Keeping and deleting

Leaderboard data stays while you play. Email me from the address below with your friend
code (Profile, at the bottom) and I'll delete your player and every score within 30 days.
Deleting the app removes the save on the phone; your iCloud copy stays in your iCloud.

## Your rights

If you're in the UK or EU you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how
it's used. Email me. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office
(ico.org.uk).
