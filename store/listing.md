## Description (4,000)

Tap to jump. Tap again to double jump. How far can your dog run?

Dobie Dash is a one-tap endless runner starring your own dog. Race through eight worlds,
from Bark Park and City Streets to Haunted Hollow and the Moon, eating biscuits, dodging
everything in your way and stomping the bosses that chase you.

MAKE IT YOUR DOG
• 14 breeds, from Dobermans and dachshunds to cockapoos and Staffies
• Pick the coat, ears, tail and eyes, and give your dog its name
• Hats, glasses, scarves, capes, collars and sparkly trails in the Wardrobe

EIGHT WORLDS, EIGHT BOSSES
• Each world has its own obstacles and a twist: waves on the beach, lamp-lit streets at
midnight, ice in the mountains, low gravity on the Moon
• Every world ends with a boss fight: dodge the attacks, then jump on its head

POWER-UPS
• Zoomies, Magnet, Shield, Frisbee Fetch and Super Sniff, each with five upgrade levels
• Save a Big Bark to clear the whole screen when things get hairy

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
• Weekly leagues: climb from Puppy to Legend against players near your level
• Daily Dash: the same course for everyone, once a day
• Weekend Cups every Friday to Sunday
• Add friends with a code, race their ghost and share a clip of your best run

EVERY DAY THERE'S SOMETHING
• Daily missions, a weekly goal, a login streak and free chests
• Pup Pass seasons with rewards to unlock
• Seasonal events, starting with Winter Walkies

No account needed. Plays offline. Game Center achievements.

Dobie Dash is free to play. It has optional in-app purchases and ads; Remove Ads takes away
the ads between runs.
